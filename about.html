OFFICIAL DISCLAIMER This application is an independent utility tool and is NOT affiliated with, authorized by, or endorsed by the Bangladesh Election Commission or any government entity. The "Voting Centers BD" app does not represent the government.

1. Introduction

We operate the Voting Centers BD mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle user information and the origin of the data provided within the app.

2. Government Data Sources

To comply with Google Play transparency policies, we declare that all government-related information (such as polling station names and locations) is retrieved from publicly available official sources:

  • Bangladesh Election Commission Portal: www.ecs.gov.bd
  • Official Government Gazettes: Publicly released polling station lists.
  • Geographic Data: Sourced via Google Maps public location data.

3. Information Collection & Use

Our app is designed as a utility to help citizens find polling stations. We prioritize user anonymity:

  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, we use GPS location to provide navigation to your center. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.
  • Personal Info: We do not collect names, NID numbers, or any personal identifiers.

We use device identifiers to improve app functionality and analyze app performance. This data is handled securely and in accordance with Google Play policies.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services for app functionality:

  • Google Play Services: For updates and app stability.
  • Google Maps: To provide route navigation.
